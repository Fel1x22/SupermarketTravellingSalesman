This is an exploration of the travelling salesman problem, framed in the context of walking around a supermarket.

When I was younger I worked at a local supermarket. One of the jobs that had to be done each night was 'putbacks', or replacing damaged products back on the store floor. This was often 
quite time consuming, and led to people taking somewhat random routes all over the store to talk to people and wind down the clock as much as possible.

This is what gave me the idea for this project, an experiment on looking for an optimal path through the store to replace all the items, starting and finishing at the staff door at the top of the store. 

I have currently included 2 premade stores, a custom store creator, and five algorithms.
These are a first route found algorithm, a brute force algorithm, an improved brute force algorithm using pruning, a greedy algorithm for a near optimal solution and finally a branch and bound implementation.
